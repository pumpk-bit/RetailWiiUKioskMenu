IN HERE PUT THE FILES THAT ARE NEEDED
=====================================

This folder is the RETAIL Wii U SLC extract (your console, not the kiosk).

1) Get otp.bin from the SAME retail Wii U (minute / NAND dump). You need it to decrypt.
2) Extract the retail SLC with NAND Extractor (needs otp.bin):
     https://github.com/koolkdev/wiiu-nandextract
   Point it at your retail slc.bin / SLC.RAW (You can safely rename the SLC.RAW to slc.bin)
   and otp.bin. Dump into THIS folder.

After extract, these paths must exist (sys\ at the top of this folder is fine;
a nested slc\ subfolder is also fine — scripts look in both):

  sys\config\system.xml
  sys\config\sys_prod.xml
  sys\rights\sys\cert.sys
  sys\rights\sys\title.list
  sys\rights\ticket\          (many .tik files)
  sys\proc\prefs\im_cfg.xml   (used by restore_im_cfg_ftp.ps1 -Mode RestoreRetail)


USA vs PAL: use the retail dump that matches YOUR console. Region is set in
config\config.ps1 (or setup_config.ps1), not by a separate folder.
